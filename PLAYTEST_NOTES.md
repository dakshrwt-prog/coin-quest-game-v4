## Ask every participant the same questions
1. Without help, how would you start and move the player?
2. Was it clear what to collect and what to avoid? What told you that?
3. When you collected a coin or got hit, was the feedback noticeable but not distracting?
4. Could you find and use pause, mute, and reduced-motion settings?
5. What was confusing, frustrating, or unfair?
6. What one change would make the game feel better?

## Tasks for each participant
- [ ] Start the game and move in four directions.
- [ ] Collect at least one coin.
- [ ] Deliberately touch a hazard if comfortable, and notice the hit feedback.
- [ ] Pause and resume using P or Escape.
- [ ] Turn mute on and off.
- [ ] Turn reduced motion on and off.
- [ ] Finish or restart a round.
